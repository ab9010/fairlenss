About FairLens

A student project about making AI fairness legible.

FairLens was developed as part of a social internship project focused on AI Ethics — Bias Detection. The goal is to make AI fairness concepts easier to understand through an interactive, accessible tool rather than a slide deck.

Project objectives

  • Create awareness about AI bias among students and the public
  • Demonstrate what fairness testing actually looks like in practice
  • Explain fairness metrics in plain, non-technical language
  • Help students understand what "responsible AI" means concretely
  • Encourage ethical habits early in an AI/ML learning journey
  • Show that fairness can be measured, not just discussed
Why this matters

Build AI that treats people fairly — and can be evaluated when it doesn't.

AI systems increasingly influence decisions that shape people's lives: who gets a loan, who gets an interview, who gets flagged as high-risk. The goal here isn't simply "build better AI." It's to build AI that treats people fairly, and to give the people building it real tools for checking that claim rather than assuming it.

Disclaimer

FairLens is an educational prototype designed to demonstrate concepts in AI fairness and bias detection. Its metrics and scoring should not be interpreted as legal, regulatory, medical, financial, or production-model certification.

The included demonstration dataset (a synthetic loan-approval dataset) is entirely synthetic and was created solely for educational purposes. It does not represent real people, real applicants, or real financial decisions.

The Fairness Score used throughout this prototype is a transparent, adjustable educational composite indicator — not a universal regulatory or scientific threshold.
